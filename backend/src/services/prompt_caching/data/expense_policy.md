# Northstar Technologies Expense and Travel Policy

**Version:** FY2026.3  
**Policy owner:** Finance Operations  
**Applies to:** employees, contractors whose agreement permits reimbursement, and approved interview candidates.

## 1. Purpose and decision standard

Northstar reimburses reasonable, necessary, business-related expenses that are supported by evidence and submitted on time. A claim is **Compliant** only when every applicable rule is satisfied. It is **Non-compliant** when a stated rule is clearly violated. It is **Needs review** when evidence is missing, a business exception may apply, the policy does not decide the case, or facts are insufficient to make a reliable decision.

Finance must not infer a missing approval, receipt, currency conversion, attendee list, business purpose, or exception. A manager may approve business need but cannot override a Finance rule unless the policy explicitly allows an exception.

## 2. Submission, evidence, and approvals

Submit claims within 60 calendar days of the transaction or return from travel, whichever is later. Claims submitted from 61 through 90 days require the employee's manager to state the reason for delay; they are Needs review until that approval is attached. Claims older than 90 days are Non-compliant unless Finance Operations documented a system-outage or legal exception before submission.

An itemized receipt is required for each individual expense of USD 25 or more (or local-currency equivalent). The receipt must show merchant, date, amount, currency, and purchased items or service. A credit-card statement alone is not an itemized receipt. For an unavailable receipt, the claimant must submit a missing-receipt declaration with merchant, date, amount, reason, and manager approval. Without all five elements, the claim is Needs review.

Every claim needs a business purpose. Client entertainment also needs attendee names, company names, and the relationship to Northstar. International travel needs an approved travel request before booking, except documented emergency travel approved by a Vice President within two business days.

## 3. Transportation and travel

Employees must use economy or standard class for air, rail, and ground travel. Premium economy is permitted on a single flight segment longer than eight hours when pre-approved by a Vice President. Business or first class is Non-compliant unless required as a documented medical accommodation approved by People Operations.

Use the lowest logical fare that reasonably meets the business schedule. A change fee is reimbursable only when caused by a business change, medical emergency, or airline cancellation. Personal itinerary changes, seat upgrades, lounge memberships, and travel insurance selected for personal reasons are Non-compliant.

Rideshare, taxi, bus, subway, parking, tolls, and standard rental cars are eligible when related to business travel. Rental-car upgrades are allowed only for required safety equipment, more than three employees traveling together, or transport of approved equipment. Fuel is reimbursable for a rental car; fuel for a personal car is not reimbursed separately when mileage is claimed.

Personal-vehicle mileage is reimbursed at USD 0.67 per business mile. Commuting between home and the normal work location is never reimbursable. Mileage logs must contain date, origin, destination, business purpose, and miles. Parking at the normal work location is Non-compliant.

## 4. Lodging

Use a standard single-occupancy room at a reasonably located business hotel. Room rate limits are defined in Appendix A. Taxes and mandatory hotel fees are eligible. Personal upgrades, minibar, movies, spa, resort fees for a leisure extension, and charges for a companion are Non-compliant.

When a trip includes personal days, only the business-night room rate and unavoidable fare difference are eligible. The claimant must provide a comparison demonstrating that the business itinerary was not made more expensive by the personal extension; otherwise the claim is Needs review.

## 5. Meals, entertainment, and alcohol

Meals while travelling overnight or more than 50 miles from the normal work location are eligible within the Appendix A daily meal limit. A meal on a one-day local trip needs a documented client, candidate, or approved team event purpose. Personal meals, groceries for home, and meals already provided by a conference, hotel, or airline are Non-compliant.

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client entertainment meal, within the total meal limit, and only with attendees and business purpose recorded. Alcohol for an internal team meal, a solo meal, or a meal without an itemized receipt is Non-compliant. Tips may not exceed 20% of the pre-tax eligible food and beverage amount unless local custom requires a documented exception.

Client entertainment is capped at USD 150 per attendee, including tax and tip, unless a Vice President pre-approved a higher amount in writing. Gifts to clients are capped at USD 75 per recipient per calendar year and may never be cash, cash equivalent, gift card, controlled item, or politically connected benefit.

When a client-entertainment claim has an itemized receipt and a stated business purpose but omits attendee names, company names, or relationship details, classify it as Needs review. Do not classify that missing-evidence case as Non-compliant unless the claim also clearly states a prohibited purpose, an over-limit amount, or another direct policy violation.

## 6. Eligible business purchases

Office supplies, approved software subscriptions, approved training, conference registration, shipping, protective equipment, and business communication costs are eligible when necessary and appropriately approved. Software or cloud subscriptions above USD 500 annually require Procurement approval before purchase. Equipment above USD 1,000 requires an approved purchase order; split transactions do not avoid this threshold.

Training or conferences above USD 2,000 require manager and Learning & Development approval before registration. Professional membership dues are eligible only if role-required or manager-approved. Internet reimbursement is limited to the monthly remote-work allowance and cannot include television, gaming, or home-equipment bundles.

## 7. Prohibited expenses

The following are always Non-compliant unless a specific written policy exception is supplied: fines, parking tickets, traffic violations, personal childcare, pet care, leisure activities, personal clothing, personal medical expenses, political contributions, donations, cash advances, cryptocurrency, weapons, tobacco, gambling, duplicate claims, and expenses paid by another organization.

## 8. Currency, tax, and exceptions

Claims in foreign currency must state the original currency and amount. Use the card-statement conversion rate when available; otherwise use the central-bank rate for the transaction date and attach the source. Finance may request review when the exchange-rate evidence is absent.

An exception request must state the rule, business reason, requested amount, and approving authority. Finance Operations approves exceptions up to USD 500; a Vice President approves USD 501 through USD 5,000; the CFO approves above USD 5,000. An exception approval after purchase is valid only for emergencies and must explain why prior approval was impossible.

## 9. Required response format for the policy checker

Return JSON only: `{"classification":"Compliant|Non-compliant|Needs review","explanation":"brief reason","policy_references":["section numbers"]}`. Cite the specific rule. Do not invent missing facts or approvals.
